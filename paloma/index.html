Interactive product prototype

Paloma

Your taste in places to drink. A personal place diary, social taste graph, and decision tool for going out.

1 · Places, not drinksThe object is the establishment: cocktail bar, dive, wine bar, brewery, lounge. Do not become Untappd.
2 · Taste, not Yelp reviewsFast reaction + contextual ranking creates a personal ordered list without forcing fake 4.3-star precision.
3 · Social utility, not a feedFriends make discovery better. The recurring utility is deciding where to go, especially as a group.
4 · Gamify explorationRankings, neighborhoods, lists, taste compatibility. Never streaks, drinks consumed, or alcohol-volume badges.
Use the phone navigation. Tap venue cards and Rate. Prototype data is illustrative.

The winning loop

Before → after

Before: “Where should we go?” → personalized and group-match discovery.

After: 5-second log → experiences → optional 1–3 comparisons → personal ranking improves.

The better the taste graph becomes, the better the next decision gets.

Classification

Primary Type · one core identity

Cocktail Bar, Dive Bar, Wine Bar, Beer Bar, Sports Bar, Pub, Lounge, Nightclub, Brewery/Taproom.

Setting · factual context

Rooftop, Hotel, Waterfront, Restaurant-attached, Outdoor/Garden, Basement, Brewery premises.

Experience · community-derived

Date night, Groups, Low-key, Dancing, Conversation, Late night, Great views, Worth the $$$, etc.

V1 boundary

  • Launch with dense SF bar coverage first.
  • Wineries/tasting rooms fit the model, but are expansion—not the launch wedge.
  • No long-form review dependency, generic social feed, live crowd surveillance, or drink-consumption game.
